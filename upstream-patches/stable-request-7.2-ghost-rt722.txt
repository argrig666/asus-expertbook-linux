Subject: Request: 7.2.y backport of three soundwire ghost-Realtek quirks

Hello,

Please backport these three commits from 7.3-rc1 to 7.2.y:

  90af3209742d ("soundwire: dmi-quirks: Disable ghost Realtek on Asus Expertbook")
  ca02ffd4975c ("soundwire: dmi-quirks: Disable ghost Realtek on Asus Zenbook Duo")
  5b08a3afd299 ("soundwire: dmi-quirks: Disable ghost Realtek on Asus ROG Zephyrus Duo")

Why: on these laptops the firmware describes an RT722 that is not fitted.
On 7.2 the SOF SoundWire machine driver then creates a duplicate
SDW3-Playback-SimpleJack link, sof_sdw fails with -EEXIST / -12, and no ALSA
card is registered at all, so the machines have no audio on any 7.2.y
kernel:

  sysfs: cannot create duplicate filename '/devices/pci0000:00/0000:00:1f.3/sof_sdw/SDW3-Playback-SimpleJack'
  kobject: kobject_add_internal failed for SDW3-Playback-SimpleJack with -EEXIST, ...
  sof_sdw sof_sdw: probe with driver sof_sdw failed with error -12

The same fix for a fourth ASUS model, 6d49beec658f ("soundwire: dmi-quirks:
Disable ghost Realtek on Asus GX651AX"), carried
"Fixes: 45cf24da0a10 ("ASoC: Intel: soc-acpi-intel-ptl-match: Remove
unnecessary cs42l43 match")" and "Cc: stable@vger.kernel.org # 7.2.x" and is
already in 7.2.8. The three commits above lack those tags only, so they were
never picked up.

All three apply cleanly, in this order, to drivers/soundwire/dmi-quirks.c in
v7.2.8. 90af3209742d closes the B9406CAA report in thesofproject/linux#5828.
Until it reaches 7.2.y, owners of that laptop rebuild snd-soc-sof-sdw with
an out-of-tree filter for the same unattached RT722 to get a card at all.
